## 数据 schema（v4.1）
- 选择题：`{"bank","kind":"single|multi|judge","chapter":"c1..c5|c0","q","opts":[...],"ans":[0起下标],"note","kb":"c1-1..c5-4(可选)"}`
- 名词：`{"t","d","chapter"}`
- 主观题：`{"chapter","q","a","note","bank"}`
- 真题：`{"banks":[{"bank","note","items":[{"kind":"choice|short|essay|timeline","q","opts","ans","a","chapter"}]}]}`

## 浏览器存储（localStorage）
`jl4.wrong` 错题本 / `jl4.star` 收藏夹 / `jl4.done` 记忆卡掌握 / `jl4.stats` 答题统计 / `jl4.prog` 小节进度 / `jl4.chprog` 章节练习进度 / `jl4.best` 模拟考最佳 / `jl4.set` 主题字号。
