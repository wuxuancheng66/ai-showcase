📈 范文一：Museum Visitors — 四大博物馆游客数量（1980-2015）

📌 题目类型：折线图 | 图表类型：趋势对比 | 评分：7.5+

In detail, the Egyptian Museum started at approximately 7,800 visitors in 1980 
and reached its peak at around 9,200 in 1995, before experiencing a slight drop 
to about 8,500 in 2000. However, it recovered and ended at roughly 9,500 in 2015.

Similarly, the Tokyo National Museum witnessed a marked rise from just under 
5,000 to approximately 9,500 over the same period, eventually catching up with 
the Egyptian Museum.

By contrast, the National Museum of Anthropology showed an opposite trend, 
decreasing steadily from about 4,200 to only 2,300.

Regarding the Hermitage, it hit the lowest point at around 3,200 between 1995 
and 2000, but then saw a gradual recovery to roughly 4,000 by 2015.

It is worth noting that in 2015, the Egyptian Museum and the Tokyo National 
Museum shared a similar figure at about 9,500 each.

💡 高分亮点分析

类别原文示例解析
📈 趋势动词started at / reached its peak / witnessed a marked rise / decreasing steadily / saw a gradual recovery避免重复使用同一动词，用不同搭配表达上升/下降/波动
📊 数据表达approximately / around / roughly / about / just under / only同义替换表示"大约"，避免单调；only 暗示下降幅度大
🔗 逻辑连接Similarly / By contrast / Regarding / It is worth noting that用连接词清晰区分不同博物馆的对比关系
🎯 时间描述in 1980 / between 1995 and 2000 / over the same period / by 2015时间点+时间段交叉使用，丰富句式
⭐ 亮点句型eventually catching up with分词结构作结果状语，展现句式多样性

📊 范文二：Seoul Entertainment Spending — 首尔娱乐消费（2014）

📌 题目类型：柱状图 | 图表类型：多组对比 | 评分：7.5+

The bar chart compares spending on cinema, dining out and music concerts by 
residents of four age brackets in Seoul, South Korea, in 2014.

Overall, dining out was the most popular form of entertainment across all age 
groups, whereas attending music concerts generally accounted for the smallest 
expenditure. It is also noticeable that the 40-55 age group allocated the 
highest total budget to leisure activities, while spending patterns varied 
considerably depending on the type of entertainment.

In detail, restaurant spending peaked among the 40-55 age group, at 
approximately $22 million, followed by the 26-39 group at around $18 million. 
The two remaining groups spent noticeably less, at roughly $16 million and $13 
million respectively. A somewhat different pattern was seen in cinema 
expenditure, where the 26-39 age group spent the most, at about $12.5 million, 
with the other three groups recording fairly similar figures ranging from $9 
million to $10.5 million.

Conversely, music concerts attracted the highest spending from the youngest 
group, at approximately $14 million, which was more than double the amount 
spent by the 40-55 and 56-80 groups, at around $7 million and $6.5 million 
respectively. It is worth noting that the 26-39 age group also spent 
considerably less on concerts, at roughly $12 million, despite being the 
highest spenders in the other two categories.

🏖️ 范文三：Holiday Leisure Activities — 四国游客度假休闲对比（2015）

📌 题目类型：柱状图 | 图表类型：多国多活动对比 | 评分：7.5+

The bar chart shows daily hours devoted to leisure pursuits by holidaymakers 
from China, the United States, Turkey and Brazil in Greece in August 2015.

Overall, there were considerable differences in how tourists from each country 
chose to spend their time. While Chinese visitors devoted the vast majority of 
their leisure hours to reading, Brazilian tourists favoured the beach, and 
American and Turkish visitors showed relatively balanced patterns across the 
three activities.

In detail, Chinese tourists spent by far the most time reading books, at an 
average of 8 hours per day—approximately twice as much as any other 
nationality. By stark contrast, they dedicated only around 3 hours to the 
beach and a mere 1 hour to visiting places, both of which were the lowest 
figures among the four groups.

A markedly different pattern was seen among Brazilian tourists, who spent the 
longest time at the beach, at roughly 6 hours daily, while allocating about 4 
hours to reading and 2.5 hours to sightseeing. Meanwhile, American and Turkish 
visitors recorded more evenly distributed schedules. Americans spent 
approximately 5 hours reading, 4 hours at the beach and 3 hours visiting 
places, whereas Turkish tourists dedicated roughly 5 hours to the beach, 4 
hours to reading and a similar 3 hours to visiting places. It is worth noting 
that visiting places was consistently the least popular activity for all groups 
except the Americans and Turks, for whom it equalled their respective beach 
and reading times.

类别原文示例解析

📊 数据表达by far the most / approximately twice as much as / a mere 1 hour / roughly / consistentlyby far 强调程度；a mere 带情感色彩暗示"少得可怜"
🔗 逻辑连接By stark contrast / A markedly different pattern was seen among / Meanwhile / whereasBy stark contrast 比 In contrast 语气更强，适合突出差异极大的情况
🎯 对比手法both of which were the lowest figures among the four groups非限定性定语从句总结两组数据，简洁高效

🛢️ 范文四：Daily Oil Production — 四国石油产量（2000-2004）

📌 题目类型：表格 | 图表类型：时间序列+对比 | 评分：7.5+

The table outlines daily crude oil output across four African nations from 
2000 to 2004.

Overall, while Congo remained the leading producer throughout the period 
despite a steady decline, Nigeria's output fluctuated before ending at a 
similar level. Meanwhile, both Chad and Somalia emerged as new oil producers, 
with their combined output reaching a notable 100,000 barrels per day by 2004.

In detail, Congo began as the dominant producer, at 275,000 barrels per day in 
2000, but this figure fell consistently over the subsequent four years to 
reach 203,000 by 2004—a decrease of 72,000 barrels. Nigeria's production 
followed a somewhat different trajectory, declining gradually from 205,000 to 
a low of 190,000 between 2000 and 2002, after which it recovered sharply to 
213,000 in 2004, thereby overtaking Congo as the largest producer.

By contrast, Chad had no oil production whatsoever during the first three 
years, only commencing output in 2003 at 8,000 barrels per day. This figure 
then surged more than sixfold to 50,000 in 2004. A broadly similar upward 
trend was seen in Somalia, where production climbed steadily from a modest 
5,000 in 2000 to 21,000 in 2003, before experiencing an even more dramatic 
increase to 50,000 in 2004. It is worth noting that Chad and Somalia shared an 
identical production figure in 2004, and their combined output equalled 
roughly one-quarter of Nigeria's production in the same year.

类别原文示例解析
📈 趋势动词fell consistently / declining gradually / recovered sharply / surged more than sixfold / climbed steadily每个趋势都搭配不同副词，精准描述变化幅度和速度
📊 数据表达a decrease of 72,000 barrels / more than sixfold / a modest 5,000 / an even more dramatic increase / roughly one-quarter用绝对变化量、倍数、比例多种方式表达数据关系
🔗 逻辑连接Meanwhile / By contrast / A broadly similar upward trend was seen in / It is worth noting thatA broadly similar upward trend was seen in 避免重复描述，一笔带过相似趋势
🎯 对比手法followed a somewhat different trajectory / thereby overtaking Congo as the largest producerthereby overtaking 分词结构表结果，简洁有力
⭐ 亮点句型after which it recovered sharply to 213,000 in 2004after which 引导的定语从句，流畅连接前后变化

💡 趋势动词写作技巧

  • 主语多样化： 同一张图不要只用 the figure，试 spending peaked / production fell / visitor numbers witnessed
  • 动词+副词搭配： fell consistently / recovered sharply / climbed steadily / declined gradually — 副词决定变化质感
  • 名词化趋势： saw a gradual recovery / witnessed a marked rise / experienced a slight drop — 比单纯用动词更正式

💡 数据表达写作技巧

  • 同义替换是7分底线： 一篇Task 1中至少用3-4个不同的词表示"大约"
  • 情感色彩词加分： a mere 1 hour（暗示少）、a modest 5,000（暗示起点低）、only 2,300（暗示下降明显）
  • 倍数关系比绝对数字更高级： more than double 比直接说具体数字更显语言能力

💡 复杂句型使用原则

  • 不要为了复杂而复杂： 每个复杂句都要有明确的功能（对比/因果/总结/补充）
  • 简单句+复杂句交替： 一段话里2-3个简单句配1个复杂句，节奏最好
  • 分词结构是性价比最高的加分项： , thereby doing / , before doing 学起来快，用起来自然
  • 推荐优先掌握（按重要程度）： ①分词作状语 ②非限定定语从句 ③Not only倒装 ④Despite+分词
