3 Accommodation — Do you live in a house or an apartment?
4 Hobbies — What do you do in your free time?
5 Music — Do you like music? What kind of music do you listen to?
6 Sports — Do you play any sports? What sports are popular in your country?
7 Food — What kind of food do you like? Do you like cooking?

9 Travel — Do you like traveling? Where have you been recently?

      Do you work or are you a student?

      I'm currently a student at a university. I'm in my second year, so I'm pretty much in the middle of my academic journey right now.

      🎤 考官 Part 1

      What subject are you studying?

      🙋 你 2-3句 · 自然流畅

      I'm majoring in environmental science. It's a broad field that covers everything from ecology to climate change and policy.

      🎤 考官 Part 1

      Why did you choose to study this subject?

      🙋 你 2-3句 · 自然流畅

      Honestly, I've always been curious about how nature works and how we can protect it. Plus, I feel like it's a really meaningful area to study, especially given all the environmental challenges we face today.

      🎤 考官 Part 1

      What do you like about your course?

      🙋 你 2-3句 · 自然流畅

      I really enjoy the practical side of it, like doing fieldwork and lab experiments. It makes the theories feel real and much more engaging than just reading textbooks.

      🎤 考官 Part 1

      What would you like to do after you finish your course?

      🙋 你 2-3句 · 自然流畅

      Ideally, I'd like to get into environmental consulting. I think it would be great to help companies or governments make more sustainable decisions.

      🎤 考官 Part 1

      Do you prefer to study in the mornings or in the afternoons?

      🙋 你 2-3句 · 自然流畅

      Definitely mornings. I feel much more focused and energetic right after breakfast, so I can get through heavy reading or complex problems more easily.

      🎤 考官 Part 1

      Do you think you will miss being a student once you finish your studies? Why or why not?

      🙋 你 2-3句 · 自然流畅

      Yeah, I probably will, to be honest. I think I'll miss the flexibility and the constant learning atmosphere, not to mention the social life with classmates.

      🎤 考官 Part 1

      What technology do you use when you study?

      🙋 你 2-3句 · 自然流畅

      I mainly use my laptop for research, writing essays, and watching online lectures. I also use some data analysis software for my science projects.

      🎤 考官 Part 1

      Are you enjoying your studies at this moment? Why or why not?

      🙋 你 2-3句 · 自然流畅

      Yes, I am, actually. I'm finding my current modules really interesting, and I feel like I'm learning things that actually matter in the real world.
