📚 新单词

单词 释义 例句
ecosystem 生态系统 The Amazon rainforest is a vital ecosystem.
biodiversity 生物多样性 Deforestation threatens biodiversity.
conservation 保护（自然/资源） Wildlife conservation is crucial.
sustainability 可持续性 We need to focus on sustainability.
mitigate 缓解，减轻 mitigate the effects of climate change
renewable 可再生的 renewable energy sources like solar and wind
depletion 消耗，枯竭 ozone depletion / resource depletion

🏗️ 新句型

句型 结构 例句
seem + 过去分词 seem + past participle The situation seems improved compared to last year.
seem + to do seem + infinitive The government seems to have taken action.
suffer from + 名词 suffer from + noun Many ecosystems suffer from pollution.
play a pivotal role in play a pivotal role in + noun/doing Education plays a pivotal role in sustainable development.
