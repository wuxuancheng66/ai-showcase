increase vs decrease vs decline increase=数量增加；decrease=数量减少（及物/不及物两用）；decline=质量/健康衰退（大多不及物），也可表示"婉拒" The price increased. / We decreased costs. / His health declined. / She declined my invitation.
seem vs seem to be seem+形容词=强调状态（简洁）；seem to be+形容词=强调客观事实（更正式） He seems happy. / He seems to be happy. / People seem attracted vs seem to be attracted
drop vs fall vs decline drop=掉落/下降（可具体可抽象）；fall=自然下落；decline=衰退/下降（更正式） He dropped his phone. / Prices fell. / The economy declined.
uncanny /ʌnˈkæni/ 离奇的，不可思议的 She had an uncanny resemblance to someone I knew.
asperity /əˈsperəti/ 粗鲁，刻薄 There was a touch of asperity in his tone.

🟡 第六类：易混淆词辨析

易混词对 区别 例句
affect vs effect affect=动词"影响"；effect=名词"效果" The policy affected many people. / The effect was significant.
principal vs principle principal=校长/主要的；principle=原则 The principal reason is cost. / I believe in this principle.
compliment vs complement compliment=赞美；complement=补充 She complimented my work. / Wine complements the meal.
stationary vs stationery stationary=静止的；stationery=文具 The car remained stationary. / I bought some stationery.
practice vs practise practice=名词；practise=动词（英式） Practice makes perfect. / I need to practise more.
