🎯 7分必备语法结构

① 定语从句

例句 说明
People who are well-educated tend to have better career prospects. 限定性定语从句
The government has introduced new policies, which are designed to reduce carbon emissions. 非限定性定语从句，对主句补充说明

② 分词结构

例句 说明
Recognizing the severity of the problem, the authorities took immediate action. 现在分词做状语（表原因）
The data collected from the survey reveals a worrying trend. 过去分词做定语（表被动）

③ 倒装句

例句 说明
Not only does education provide knowledge, but it also fosters critical thinking. "Not only... but also" 倒装
Only through international cooperation can we effectively address climate change. "Only + 状语" 倒装
Hardly had the policy been introduced when it faced widespread criticism. "Hardly... when" 倒装

④ 虚拟语气

例句 说明
Without government intervention, environmental degradation would accelerate. 假设法
It is high time that governments took stronger measures to tackle pollution. It's high time + 虚拟过去时
If more resources were allocated to education, the outcomes would be significantly better. 与现在事实相反

⑤ 名词性从句

例句 说明
What the government should do is to invest more in public infrastructure. 主语从句
It is widely believed that education is the key to success. 形式主语
The fact that climate change is accelerating is beyond dispute. 同位语从句

🎯 系动词与易混动词辨析

📌 来源：你与AI的深度学习对话，重点解决实际使用中的困惑

① Seem 的用法（系动词核心规则）

结构 说明 例句
seem + 形容词/过去分词 强调当前状态（简洁有力） People seem attracted by the offer. / She seems happy. / He seems tired.
seem to be + 形容词/过去分词 强调客观事实（语气更委婉正式） People seem to be attracted by the offer. / The plan seems to be doomed to fail.
seem + 名词 后接名词时必须加to be He seems to be a nice person. ✅ / He seems a nice person. ❌（口语中有时可接受，但考试不推荐）

💡 记忆口诀：Seem 是"状态词"，后面直接跟"状态"（形容词）；"to be"是可选套餐，加不加全看你想不想把语气说得更"端着"一点。

同类词延伸：appear, look 也有类似用法：She looks tired. / She appears convinced.

② Drop 的过去式与过去分词

原形 过去式 过去分词 规则 例句
drop dropped dropped 辅音+元音+辅音结尾，双写p再加-ed He dropped his phone yesterday. / The vase was dropped.

💡 注意：dropped 也可用作形容词，表示"掉落的/被取消的"，如 a dropped call（断线的电话）、dropped charges（被撤销的指控）。这延续了"seem + 过去分词"的用法：He seems dropped from the team.

③ Increase / Decrease / Decline 的过去式与过去分词

原形 过去式 过去分词 说明
increase increased increased 数量上的增加，及物/不及物两用
decrease decreased decreased 数量上的减少，及物/不及物两用
decline declined declined 质量/健康/重要性的衰退，大多不及物；也可表示"婉拒"

💡 关键区别：

  • increase / decrease：强调数量上的增减，可及物可不及物：The price increased.（不及物）/ We increased the price.（及物）
  • decline：偏向质量、健康、重要性的衰退，大多不及物：His health declined. ✅ 不能说 We declined the price. ❌（此处应用 decreased 或 reduced）
  • decline 的特殊用法：表示"婉拒"（及物，相当于 politely refuse）：She declined my invitation.
  • reduce：及物动词，表示"人为降低"：We reduced the price.（强调主动行为）

💡 写作应用：这三个词都可以用作"seem + 过去分词"表状态：The risk seems increased. / Sales seem decreased. / His interest seems declined.（但更地道的说法是 Sales seem to have decreased）
